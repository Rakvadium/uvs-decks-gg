## Formats Overview

Formats dictate deck size, card pool, and deckbuilding rules. Full competitive documents: [Rules and Documents](https://uvsgames.com/universus/organized-play/rules-and-documents/).

| Format | Card pool (summary) | Min deck |
|--------|---------------------|----------|
| **Standard** | Mechanically unique cards from the current year + past two years (except banned) | 1 starting character, 60 cards, optional 0 or 10 sideboard |
| **Hero Too** | All cards from My Hero Academia Base Booster Set through current release; Standard bans + additional Hero Too bans | Same as Standard |
| **Retro** | All UVS Games UniVersus cards ever printed | Same as Standard |
| **Spotlight Constructed** | Only cards from a chosen IP/world (reprints legal if they appeared in that world, including promos) | Same as Standard |
| **Sealed** | Six booster packs; build from opened pool | 1 starting character, 40 cards; rest of pool = sideboard |
| **Booster Draft** | Six packs drafted | 1 starting character, 40 cards; undrafted picks = sideboard |

**Format prefixes** (can attach to any legal format):

- **Teams** — 3-player teams, best-of-3 vs another team; no shared characters; team members may not share the same Primary Symbol (Attuned from another member’s Primary OK). Starting characters revealed simultaneously; matchups picked with random first pick.
- **3v3** — one player, three characters (Point / Main / Anchor). Deck must be legal for all three. On character swap, combat ends; keep up to 8 non-character stage cards and sacrifice the rest. On cycle, lose 2 health at end of turn per cycle instead of removing 10. FAQ: [UniVersus 3v3 Match Mode](https://uvsgames.com/news/universus-3v3-match-mode/).

**Sealed extras:** symbol restrictions removed; cycle removes 5 instead of 10.  
**Draft extras:** “Modified Draft” (no symbols) or traditional (one symbol must match character); cycle removes 5.

> Formats **not** currently published as separate living banlists on the official hub: Legacy, Expanded, Banless, Challenge. Spotlight is IP-specific; only the My Hero Academia Spotlight ban list is published on the Formats page.

## Card Errata

An errata is an official change to a card’s printed text (rebalance, misprint fix, clarification, or error correction). Always play the latest errata in every format.

Official groupings on the Formats page:

| Category | Meaning (as framed by UVS) |
|----------|----------------------------|
| **Power Level Errata** | Rebalancing / competitive power adjustments |
| **Functional Errata** | Correcting how the card works / misprints affecting function |
| **Clarifying Errata** | Clarifications, reminder text, symbols, collector fixes |
| **Non-Standard Format – Errata List** | Errata primarily relevant outside Standard’s current pool |

## Alternate Art, Chrome Rare & Reprints

### Alternate Art Cards
Alternate-art versions of a standard-issue card may be used as copies of that card. This does **not** allow more than 4 copies of a single card name in a deck.

### Chrome Rare Cards
Serialized unique-foil versions of standard-issue cards may be used as copies of that card. Same 4-copy limit applies.

### Reprinted Cards
When cards share the same name as a previous UniVersus product, and symbols, difficulty, and check are the same, and game text is identical or nearly identical, they are the same card. Terminology updates (e.g. “vitality” → “health”, “add this card to your staging area” → “build it”) are **not** different functionality. If an old printing lacked a block and a reprint has one, all versions are considered to possess that block.
